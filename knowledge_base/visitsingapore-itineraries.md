Fun for the Family

Disappearing Trades
Disappearing Trades
Take a trip down memory lane by learning about Singapore's fast-disappearing old-school businesses. You will visit a traditional bakery, an ancestral paper money shop and a coffee roasting factory.

Little India Heritage Trail - Walk of Faiths
Little India Heritage Trail - Walk of Faiths
Explore Little India at your own pace with this trail. It takes you past temples, mosques, and churches that stand just a few streets apart, reflecting the neighbourhood's rich tapestry of faiths.

Henderson Waves
Henderson Waves
Venture above the tree line and admire the view of the surrounding greenery from the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore.

Central Catchment Reserve
Central Catchment Reserve
Spanning over 2,000 hectares of forest with more than 20km of trails, the Central Catchment Nature Reserve is Singapore's largest nature reserve. Highlights include the Treetop Walk's panoramic views and the calm waters of MacRitchie Reservoir.

Queenstown Heritage Trail
Queenstown Heritage Trail
Wander past old landmarks and modern redevelopments of Singapore's first satellite town. Uncover stories of how this once-quiet estate became a living record of our city's growth.

Singapore Botanic Gardens
Singapore Botanic Gardens
This UNESCO World Heritage Site is the perfect spot for a picnic, a jog or a reprieve from the buzz of the city.

Coast-to-Coast Trail
Coast-to-Coast Trail
Stretching 36km across Singapore, this trail links nature areas, parks, and park connectors from Jurong Lake Gardens to Coney Island. This rewarding trail can also be enjoyed in parts, from a leisurely evening walk to a scenic park connector ride.

Nowhere Baths
Nowhere Baths
A contemporary gender-neutral bathhouse, Nowhere Baths offers hot baths, cold plunges, a sauna, and a steam room. Each experience is designed to soothe muscles, quiet the mind, and promote recovery.

Tea Chapter
Tea Chapter
Tea Chapter's appreciation workshops offer solo travellers a meditative journey into Chinese tea culture. As Singapore's largest and oldest teahouse, it invites you to slow down and embrace the calm ritual of brewing and savouring tea in its most authentic form.

Gentle Walks
Gentle Walks
Gentle Walks leads you through forest therapy around Singapore's beautiful parks, using mindful invitations and unhurried wandering to help you reconnect with nature and yourself.

Rail Corridor
Rail Corridor

Naturalist Night Adventure

Artyzen Singapore
A sanctuary in the city, Artyzen offers solo female travellers wellness and workout initiatives designed for solo rejuvenation. Enjoy a morning yoga class, take a swim in the rooftop "floating pool" which features a partial glass bottom, or indulge in gemstone-infused spa rituals.

Places to Visit in Singapore with Your Family
Travelling with family? Here's our recommended things to do and experiences that can't be missed for the best of Singapore fit for all ages.

Day 1
Soak in the city views and visit Singapore's most iconic family spots.

Singapore DuckTours
Spend your first day in Singapore sampling all that our city has to offer with this hop-on and hop-off ride. Some highlights include Little India, Orchard Road and Chinatown.

National Gallery
Visit the world's largest public display of modern Southeast Asian art. Stroller friendly, it also houses the Keppel Centre for Art Education, a children-centric learning zone that revolves around the themes of art and wonder.

ArtScience Museum
This beloved lotus-inspired establishment blurs the lines between art, science, culture and technology, with exciting hands-on and interactive exhibitions on rotation that are perfect for children.

Day 2
Discover heart-pumping thrills and a dose of discovery.

Sentosa
Spend the whole day at Sentosa, a playground for the whole family. There’s something for everyone on this island at Southern Singapore, including a giant floating water park - Hydrodash, with designated zones for kids.

Universal Studios Singapore
This movie-themed entertainment park is peppered with thrilling rides based on blockbuster flicks like Transformers and Jurassic Park.

Skyline Luge
Feel your adrenaline pumping while riding this 3-wheeled luge carts down windy tracks, that's great for both the young and young at heart.

Day 3
Get closer to nature, indulge your sweet tooth, and shop to your heart's content. 

Singapore Botanic Gardens
Take a stroll at Singapore’s first UNESCO World Heritage Site. Check out highlights like the Swan Lake and National Orchid Garden, which is home to the world’s largest display of orchids.

Jacob Ballas Children's Gardens
The first garden in Asia dedicated to children, this park allows children to explore, play and have an adventure, while learning and cultivating a love for nature.

Dempsey
Within the greenery of the Dempsey neighbourhood, you will find great spots for families to unwind - like PS. Cafe, which offers Modern European creations, and the Museum of Ice Cream, where children two and under can enter for free.

Day 4
Enjoy unforgettable wildlife encounters and kid-friendly adventures.

Singapore Zoo
Observe animals in their natural habits at the Singapore Zoo. Indulge in a hearty Breakfast in the Wild, which will have you dining alongside Orangutans and Macaws, perfect for all animal lovers.

Bird Paradise
Pop by adjacent Bird Paradise, home to over 3,500 birds, with highlights including walk through aviaries that reflect habitats across the world.

River Wonders
Alternatively, head to a river-themed zoo and aquarium to find animals like the Giant Panda, Brazilian Tapirs and Capybara.

Things You Need to Know
-

Mass Rapid Transit Map
For convenient navigation throughout the city, you may download the MyTransport App for public transport routes and timings. You may also download the Singapore MRT map, which offers a comprehensive overview of the MRT system.

Language
You can communicate in English with Singaporeans, who are mostly fluent in it. Many also speak Mandarin Chinese, Malay or Tamil. You may also encounter Singlish, a unique colloquial form of English that blends with various languages and dialects spoken in Singapore.
